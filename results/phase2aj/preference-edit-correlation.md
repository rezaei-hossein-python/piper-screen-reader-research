V6 winners were exclamation mark and expanded; both receive substantial E2 boundary/PAD edits and at least one terminal/vowel edit. V1 winners U, W and unavailable do not justify attributing any single family as harmful. Original winners S and 0 are sensitive items: even E1 may be unnecessary or preference-sensitive. No simple adaptive rule is justified from seven observations; Candidate M=A5 and Candidate F=A6 are fixed, phonetic-plan-based ablations.
